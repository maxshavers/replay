Replay

Thanks for testing Replay! This is an early prototype that helps you catch what someone just said.
  • Use Chrome on a laptop, and wear headphones.
  • Tap Start listening, let people talk, then tap Replay (or press Space) whenever you miss something.
  • Sound stays in memory on your device and only the last 15 seconds are kept. Nothing is saved. The live captions use Chrome's built-in speech service, which sends audio to Google to turn it into text.
  • Only use it in conversations where everyone knows and agrees.
  • Replay is a listening aid, not a medical device or a treatment for CAPD or hearing loss. It doesn't replace advice from an audiologist or doctor.
